Mission Object & The Loop

This panel shows bounded refinement: probabilistic generation proposes, validation emits findings, and fixed routing either permits another attempt or closes a proposal. Admission remains separate.

Deterministic Sandwich

Model / GenAI

One bounded stochastic step. Its output remains a candidate; Validation supplies evidence about declared properties.

Operational Notes

The model explores candidates inside a bounded mission packet; no side effects happen at this layer.

  • Draft outputs are proposals, not accepted state.
  • Checks can close a proposal; independent authority still controls admission.
In this minimal Sandwich, only the middle is stochastic. Larger workflows may use probabilistic evaluation inside fixed enforcement and authority boundaries.

Substrate - Governed work starts when intent, observed reality, and retained evidence are explicit.

Map

Intent surface

A structured, versioned representation of intent or observed structure, authoritative only for declared properties and scope.

Terrain

Operational reality

Reality-as-code: source, configs, schemas, and the behavior your systems actually execute.

Ledger

Reconstructable evidence

Append-only intent, input fingerprints, effects, structured results, transitions, and provenance. Under protected-sink and trust-root assumptions, missing or contradictory required evidence fails closed.

Semantic Density

Semantic Density, Correctness, and Relevance

Three independent properties of mission-ready context. Optimize all three; none can compensate for a missing one.

Semantic Density

Distinct, decision-bearing meaning per unit of context.

Correctness

Evidence-bound claims, current enough for the decision, with contradictions exposed.

Relevance

Material tied to the active Mission, property, and decision.

Dense falsehood misleads. Correct irrelevance distracts. Relevant repetition wastes attention.

Authority and Enforcement for One Property



                    Authority is declared for the property being decided. The control plane protects the comparison and transition; it does not become a universal source of truth.
                






1 / Declare


Map


Desired condition, authoritative source, owner, and transition policy.






2 / Observe


Terrain Sensor


Pinned observed state with provenance. No authority is inferred from the observation.






3 / Compare


Validator


Structured finding: pass, fail, or inconclusive, with evidence references and protocol identity.






4 / Decide


Route, Seal, Admit


Close the proposal under fixed routing; separate authority decides admission.










Ordinary Mission


May change bounded Terrain. A protected effect is denied, recorded, and ends with sealed evidence.






Governance Mission


May emit a checked proposal. A different authority applies, approves, and admits it.

 under protected-sink and trust-root assumptions, reconstructs authority, observations, effects, findings, and decisions; missing or contradictory required evidence fails closed.
