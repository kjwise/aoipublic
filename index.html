Architects of Intent

Friday afternoon, you merge an AI-assisted change. Saturday morning, your app is down. The AI quietly broke a contract your type system never saw.

This book is about making that impossible.

“We don’t trust the model. We trust the loop.”

Architects of Intent is an online book on Software Development as Code (SDaC): build reliable, auditable GenAI systems with deterministic context, gates, and verifiable loops.

Who This Is For

  • Senior engineers building AI-assisted development tools
  • Platform architects designing autonomous systems
  • Engineering leaders establishing AI governance
  • Anyone who has been burned by code that “looked fine”

What You Will Learn

  • The Deterministic Sandwich: wrap every AI call in Prep -> Model -> Validation
  • Mission Objects: turn vague prompts into typed, executable contracts
  • Validators: transform “looks fine” into deterministic PASS/FAIL
  • Circuit breakers: keep loops finite and auditable
  • Governance at scale: protect the graders from the system they grade

Why Trust This

Written by Jóhann Haukur Gunnarsson, systems architect and senior software engineer. He has built and operated reliability-critical systems, including in finance, where failure modes are not theoretical and audit trails matter.

About the Author

Jóhann is an Icelandic systems architect focused on high-reliability engineering and AI-assisted development workflows. He writes from the perspective of someone who has had to make production systems auditable, testable, and safe under real delivery pressure.
