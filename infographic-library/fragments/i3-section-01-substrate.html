The Substrate (Map · Terrain · Ledger)

SDaC is practical when the system has three explicit surfaces: intent, runnable reality, and a deterministic evidence trail.

MAP
Intent surface
Mission objects, constraints, and acceptance criteria.
TERRAIN
Runnable reality
Code, configs, schemas, and policies your system actually runs.
LEDGER
Evidence trail
Append-only records of diffs, validator output, and decisions.
