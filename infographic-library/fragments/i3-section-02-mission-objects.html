Mission Objects (Intent As Code)

A mission object is a programmable unit of work: goals, constraints, budgets, and acceptance criteria. It turns a vague request into a deterministic interface.

Why it matters
The tighter the mission boundary, the smaller the search space. That is how you get faster convergence without “trusting the model.”
