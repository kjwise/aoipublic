Intent Architecture Wins the Output War

AI output volume rises faster than humans can consume it. Competition shifts to intention architecture: converting volume into high-signal, coherent outcomes.

  • More output without intent raises review and integration load.
  • Stronger intent boosts signal density and coherence.
  • High-signal loops compound; low-signal loops stall.
Executive takeaway: The bottleneck isn’t output. It’s the ability to consume, validate, and integrate the right output.
