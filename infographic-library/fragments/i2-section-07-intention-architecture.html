Micro-Bridge: One Block + One Gate

The smallest Hofstadter Bridge is one bounded Map heading that is regenerated from Terrain and validated as a hard gate.

Deterministic micro-loop
1) Extract skeleton facts from Terrain.
2) Sync one heading block (diff-first).
3) Validate exact Map/Terrain match.
4) Gate merge on PASS/FAIL.
Why this crosses the Bridge: drift is no longer advisory text debt; it becomes a deterministic CI failure.
