The Fractal Nature

Zoom in: a single function evolves through edits. Zoom out: a company evolves through quarters.

It is the same shape at every scale. Each level's "state" is the cumulative output of the faster iterations beneath it. Dysfunction at the bottom bubbles up.

next_state = process(current_state)
process = improve(process, evidence)
