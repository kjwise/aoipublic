Ratchets (Quality Floors That Only Move Up)

Ratchets convert progress into non-regression law. A candidate may fail, but the floor does not move down. When a candidate passes the gate, the floor is raised and future loops compound from a better baseline.

Ratchet conditions
quality(Zn) >= quality(Zn-1) mnew >= mold Here m is any tracked metric (for example reliability, velocity, defensibility, compliance). q(Z) = Σ wi · mi(Z), with Σ wi = 1 FAIL does not lower the floor. PASS can raise it.
