The Fractal Nature of Work

Same loop, different cadence. Historically, a function evolved in minutes; a company evolved in years. AI compresses the lower-loop cadences first, but organizational loops still move slower. Each level’s state is the accumulated output of the faster loops beneath it.

  • Bottom-layer drift multiplies upward.
  • Bottom-layer improvements compound upward.
  • Intent and validation at every scale keep the loop coherent.

2) Sync one heading block (diff-first).
3) Validate exact Map/Terrain match.
4) Gate merge on PASS/FAIL.
Why this crosses the Bridge: drift is no longer advisory text debt; it becomes a deterministic CI failure.
